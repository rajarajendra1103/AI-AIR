🔄 End-to-End System Processing Pipeline
📍
1. City Input & Geocoding Query
User submits global city query ➔ Open-Meteo Geocoding API resolves to latitude/longitude coordinates.
🌐
2. Open-Meteo Live Telemetry & 14-Day Resampling
Synchronous retrieval of 7 criteria pollutants (PM2.5, PM10, NO2, SO2, CO, O3, NH3) and 5 meteorological variables.
📐
3. CPCB Sub-Index Calculation Engine
Computes piecewise linear sub-indices across all 7 pollutants; identifies the primary dominant driver.
🧠
4. PyTorch Deep Neural Multi-Step Forecaster
Input sequence [14, 10] passed through GRU / LSTM / Transformer to evaluate multi-step forecast trajectory.
🩺
5. Weather-Aware AI Health Advisory Agent
Multi-variable sensitivity scoring model synthesizes clinical guidance, mask directives, and purifier recommendations.
🤖 PyTorch Deep Learning Model Architectures
LSTM Forecaster (Stacked)
2-Layer Stacked Long Short-Term Memory network capturing long-range atmospheric temporal dependencies.
Input: [Batch, 14, 10]
➔ LSTM(hidden=64, num_layers=2)
➔ Dropout(0.2) + Linear Head
➔ Output: Forecast AQI [Batch, 1]
⭐ GRU Forecaster (Best Performer)
2-Layer Gated Recurrent Unit with reset and update gates offering superior generalization and lowest inference latency.
Input: [Batch, 14, 10]
➔ GRU(hidden=64, num_layers=2)
➔ Hidden State h_t [Batch, 64]
➔ Output: Forecast AQI [Batch, 1]
Transformer Forecaster
Multi-Head Self-Attention encoder capturing complex inter-pollutant atmospheric cross-correlations.
Input: [Batch, 14, 10]
➔ PositionalEncoding + Linear(64)
➔ TransformerEncoder(nhead=4, dim=128)
➔ Output: Forecast AQI [Batch, 1]
📖 Interactive Air Quality & Meteorological Data Dictionary

Click on any parameter below to expand scientific definitions, measurement units, and CPCB safe standard thresholds.

🌫️ PM2.5 (Fine Particulate Matter ≤ 2.5 µm) ▼
Unit: µg/m³ • CPCB Safe Limit (24-hr): 60 µg/m³
Combustion particles, organic compounds, metals. Microscopic particles capable of penetrating deep into lungs and entering the bloodstream.
🌪️ PM10 (Coarse Inhalable Particulate Matter ≤ 10 µm) ▼
Unit: µg/m³ • CPCB Safe Limit (24-hr): 100 µg/m³
Crushed stone, road dust, construction debris, agricultural soot. Inhaled into upper respiratory tract.
🚗 NO2 (Nitrogen Dioxide) ▼
Unit: µg/m³ • CPCB Safe Limit (24-hr): 80 µg/m³
Emitted primarily from internal combustion vehicle engines and power plants; contributes to ground-level ozone formation and smog.
🏭 SO2 (Sulphur Dioxide) ▼
Unit: µg/m³ • CPCB Safe Limit (24-hr): 80 µg/m³
Produced by industrial fossil fuel combustion and refining; triggers severe bronchoconstriction in asthmatic individuals.
🔥 CO (Carbon Monoxide) ▼
Unit: mg/m³ • CPCB Safe Limit (8-hr): 2.0 mg/m³
Colorless, odorless toxic gas reducing oxygen delivery to heart and brain tissue by forming carboxyhemoglobin.
☀️ O3 (Ground-Level Ozone) ▼
Unit: µg/m³ • CPCB Safe Limit (8-hr): 100 µg/m³
Secondary photochemical pollutant formed when NOx and volatile organic compounds (VOCs) react under intense sunlight and high temperature.
🧪 NH3 (Ammonia) ▼
Unit: µg/m³ • CPCB Safe Limit (24-hr): 400 µg/m³
Emitted from agricultural fertilizer application and livestock waste; precursor to secondary inorganic aerosol formation.
